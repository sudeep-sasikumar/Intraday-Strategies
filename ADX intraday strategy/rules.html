Uses the DMI indicator (14, 14) on the 15-minute chart. It draws three lines: DI+, DI− and ADX. The ADX number (20, 40…) is ignored. Only where the ADX line sits matters.

  1. Trend. DI+ above DI− = up, look for buys. DI− above DI+ = down, look for sells.
  2. Setup. The ADX line was below both DI lines and now closes between them, and it is rising.
  3. Entry. Buy if the very next candle breaks the setup candle's high (sell if it breaks the low). If it doesn't, the setup is dropped.
  4. Stop loss. The other end of the setup candle: its low for a buy, its high for a sell.
  5. Exit. No fixed target. Get out when the ADX line closes above both DI lines (strength used up).
  6. Skip. If ADX comes in between the DI lines from above, no trade.

Filled in by me (the video doesn't say)

  • No new entries after 14:45. Everything still open is closed at 15:15. Nothing is carried overnight.
  • Also exit if DI+ and DI− swap places (the trend flipped).
  • One trade per stock at a time.

Optional changes (Settings tab, all off by default)

  • Stop loss rule = atr. Stop a fixed distance from the entry (default 2 × the average 15-minute candle range) instead of the setup candle's other end. Fewer stop-outs.
  • Exit when ADX goes above both DI = off. Hold to the stop or the 15:15 square-off.
  • Profit target. Take profit at a multiple of the stop distance.
  • Market-wide burst. Take a setup only when many stocks show a setup in the same direction in the same candle, and the market has not already moved that way.
  • Most trades open at once. For example 2.
