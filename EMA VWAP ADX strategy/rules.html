Uses the 5-minute chart with four lines: 9 EMA, 21 EMA, VWAP (resets every day) and ADX 14. The idea: a 9/21 crossover alone gives many false signals, so it is only traded when VWAP and ADX agree.

  1. Crossover. 9 EMA crosses above the 21 EMA = look to buy. Crosses below = look to sell.
  2. VWAP. For a buy the candle must close above VWAP; for a sell, below it.
  3. ADX. ADX must be above 20 on that candle, for buys and for sells (it measures strength, not direction).
  4. Wait if needed. If the crossover candle fails VWAP or ADX, keep waiting. The first later candle that passes everything is the confirmation candle. Its colour does not matter.
  5. Entry. When the confirmation candle closes (filled at the next candle's open).
  6. Stop loss. The 21 EMA's value on the confirmation candle.
  7. Take profit. Twice the stop distance (risk 1 to make 2). The video's alternative: hold until the opposite crossover (a setting).
